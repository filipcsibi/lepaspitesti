# Cookie policy

The site lepaspitesti.ro uses cookies to analyse how you reach the platform, to understand browsing behaviour and to optimise how the site works. The aim is to offer you an experience that is as relevant and efficient as possible.

It is important to note that these cookies do not collect personal information that identifies users individually. The data is used exclusively for statistical and functional purposes. You can also set your browser to block cookies or to notify you when they are used.

This page explains how LEPAS Pitești uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your online experience.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you access a website. They allow the device to be recognised and remember your preferences or actions for a set period.

## Why do we use cookies?

We use cookies to:

- improve the performance of the site;
- personalise content;
- analyse traffic;
- provide relevant advertising, including through third-party cookies.

## Types of cookies used

- **Session cookies** – Deleted automatically when you close the browser. They keep temporary functions working.
- **Persistent cookies** – Stay on the device until they expire or are deleted manually.
- **Third-party cookies** – Placed by external services (e.g. Google, Meta, TikTok) for analytics or advertising.
- **Cookies with a variable lifetime** – May be deleted when you close the browser or kept temporarily, depending on the context.

## Managing cookies

You have full control over cookies from your browser settings. Please note that switching off certain cookies may affect how well the site works.

For more information about managing cookies, visit: [www.aboutcookies.org](http://www.aboutcookies.org) or [www.allaboutcookies.org](http://www.allaboutcookies.org)
